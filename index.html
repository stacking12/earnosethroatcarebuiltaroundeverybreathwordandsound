FAQs

Common Questions About Our Otolaryngologist Care

Answers to what new patients most often ask before their first visit.

What does an Otolaryngologist treat?

An Otolaryngologist, also known as an Ear Nose Throat Doctor, diagnoses and treats conditions affecting the ears, nose, sinuses, and throat — including ear infections, sinus congestion, tonsil and adenoid issues, hearing loss, snoring, and sleep apnea.

Do you see children as well as adults?

Yes. We regularly treat children for ear infections, tonsil and adenoid concerns, and speech-delay ENT evaluations, alongside adult patients dealing with sinus, hearing, or sleep-related issues.

How do I know if I need a Sinus Specialist?

If you experience ongoing nasal congestion, facial pressure, or sinus infections that keep returning, it's worth having an evaluation. Our Otolaryngologist can determine whether the cause is structural, allergy-related, or infectious.

Can snoring or sleep apnea actually be treated here?

Yes. We evaluate snoring and sleep apnea symptoms, identify airway or nasal factors that may be contributing, and recommend a therapy plan suited to your situation.

Call our Brooklyn office directly at 347-428-7916, or visit our website to request an appointment online. New patients are welcome.
